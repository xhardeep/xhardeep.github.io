date: "2026-05-27"
---

Humans are wired to like—and even love—those who treat them well. This isn’t just sentiment; it’s a **biological conditioning mechanism** that reshapes cognition and behavior.

Liking and loving set up the trap for us like
1. **Blind Spots:** We overlook flaws in those we admire, often complying with their wishes without question.
2. **Halo Effect:** We favor people, products, or ideas simply because they’re linked to someone we love.
3. **Reality Distortion:** We twist facts to justify our affection, even when the truth is inconvenient.

Admiration fuels love, and love amplifies admiration. This cycle can spiral into extremes—sometimes even self-destruction—for the sake of the loved object.

To avoid this trap what you should do is-
- Adopt the prescription: **"See it like it is and love anyway."**
- Recognize that love causes a "blindness" to objective reality.
- When faced with a choice involving someone you care about, ask yourself how you will feel about the situation in minutes, months, and years.
- **Focus on Actions, Not Words:** Hold people accountable to their behavior instead of potential. 

When directed toward *truly* admirable figures, this tendency becomes a superpower, driving growth, loyalty, and success.
